Explore the dungeon. Master your Nen. Earn your Hunter License.

How to Play

  1. Goal: Clear 8 dungeon rooms, get the Hunter License Key, defeat the Elite Nen Guardian, then escape through the Exit.
  2. Explore each room to reveal enemies, items, or bonus gold.
  3. When an enemy is present, press Fight and pick an action: Normal Attack, Nen Attack, Defend, Use Potion, or Run.
  4. Winning battles gives you gold and XP. Level up to raise HP, Attack, and Nen Power.
  5. A room must be explored (and its enemy beaten) before the Next Room portal unlocks.
  6. Use Nen to boost your stats, and Items to heal or equip gear.
  7. You lose if your HP reaches 0.
