# Five-Member Defense Plan

## Member 1 — Game Flow
Files: `src/main.js`, `src/game/Game.js`
Focus: startup, menu, game loop, win/lose logic.

## Member 2 — Player
File: `src/characters/Player.js`
Focus: player properties, constructor, HP, XP, level, Nen, methods.

## Member 3 — Combat
Files: `src/characters/Enemy.js`, `src/combat/CombatSystem.js`
Focus: attacks, enemy turns, critical hits, battle loop.

## Member 4 — Dungeon
Files: `src/world/Room.js`, `src/world/Dungeon.js`
Focus: room objects, map, room progression, portal rules.

## Member 5 — Items
Files: `src/items/Item.js`, `src/items/Inventory.js`
Focus: item objects, inventory array, add/remove, map/filter/reduce.
